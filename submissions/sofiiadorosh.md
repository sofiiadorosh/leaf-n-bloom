## About the app

Leaf & Bloom is an online houseplant store. Shoppers can browse the catalog,
search by plant name, filter by plant type, light needs, pet safety and price,
sort by price, and share the current view through the URL. Each product opens
a details drawer with a photo gallery, care guide, size options and extra care.
The cart saves sizes, extra care and quantities across pages and reloads, and
supports the `SALE10` promo code.
